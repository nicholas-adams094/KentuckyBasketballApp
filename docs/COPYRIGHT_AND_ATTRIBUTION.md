## Current status: NOT CLEARED FOR PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION

All 70 entries in `src/data/photo-manifest.json` carry
`rights_review_status: "required-before-publication"`. No image in this repository has
been cleared for redistribution.

The build reflects that:

- `public/robots.txt` disallows all crawling.
- `index.html` sets `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">`.
- The Sources view states the position prominently to any reader.

Do not remove those safeguards until the review below is complete.

## Before any public deployment

1. **Review every image individually.** For each manifest entry, establish the copyright
   holder, the licence or permission that applies, and whether the intended use is
   covered. Record the outcome in the manifest.
2. **Preserve required attribution.** Where a licence requires credit, it must appear in
   the Sources view alongside the existing photo credits.
3. **Remove or replace what cannot be used.** An image whose status cannot be established
   is not cleared by default. Replace it, or fall back to the generated jersey card —
   which the archive already does for players with no verified image.
4. **Confirm no implied endorsement.** The four team-photograph crops, the one
   jersey-card entry and the two out-of-era portraits must remain visibly flagged, and nothing in the presentation may suggest official
   University of Kentucky imagery or approval.
5. **Update this document and the manifest**, then remove `robots.txt` and the `noindex`
   meta as a deliberate, separate change.

## Text and data

Historical statistics and results are facts, compiled from the sources listed in
`archive.json` and shown in the Sources view. The editorial writing — season stories,
player scouting summaries, signature-moment framing — is original to this project.

Derived metrics (impact rating, lineup rating, era percentiles, the all-decade five) are
computed by this application and are clearly labelled as derived. They are not official
statistics and must never be presented as such.

## Scope of this document

This is a working checklist, not a legal clearance determination. The photo manifest
tracks source and review status; it does not establish rights. Obtain qualified advice
before publishing.
